---
title: Sharing
description: Plan links that let other people open a project.
---

Project sharing by preview link or QR code is a product capability to implement. The current project does not create share links or manage preview access.

## Decide who can open the link

Choose whether a preview is public, requires sign-in, or is limited to specific people. Enforce that choice on the server.

A QR code contains a URL. It does not add an access rule.

## Share the right version

Make it clear whether a link opens a changing preview or a published release.

## Review

Open the link in a separate signed-out browser and check the expected access. For mobile links, also check what happens with and without the app installed.

See [permissions](/docs/authentication/permissions).
