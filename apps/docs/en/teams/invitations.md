---
title: Invitations
description: Invite people to a shared account.
---

The backend includes operations to list, send, resend, and revoke team invitations.

## Send an invitation

A team administrator supplies the invitee's email and role. The server uses the active organization rather than an organization chosen by the browser.

## Manage pending invitations

Resend and revoke operations check that the invitation belongs to the active team and is still pending.

Keep pending invitations separate from active members in the interface. Sending an invitation does not make the person an active member.

## Before launch

Connect the invitation operations to app screens. Check delivery, acceptance, and membership in your WorkOS environment, including what happens when someone follows an old invitation.
